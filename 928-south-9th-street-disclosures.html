Risk radar

這些不是「一定不能買」的訊號，而是最值得在 contingency 期內用專業報價、保險確認、written request 去釐清的地方。

Priority 01

Electrical and life-safety items need a real electrician.

  • Main/sub panels: inspector flags older electrical service and possible problem panel brands.
  • Attic: exposed live wires, missing junction box covers, and splices outside junction boxes.
  • Smoke detector did not operate; CO detector missing at hall.
Priority 02

Roof, eaves, fascia, and wood-destroying organisms overlap.

  • Termite report notes drywood termite evidence and damage at eaves/fascia, door jamb, and attic wood frame.
  • Dry rot/fungus appears at eaves rafter tail and door frame/jamb.
  • Roof materials are described as nearing end of useful life with damaged/missing shingles and ponding history.
Priority 03

Foundation and drainage are old-house negotiation items.

  • Foundation walls show efflorescence, indicating past moisture accumulation.
  • No anchor bolts, typical for pre-1935 homes, but seismic retrofit is recommended.
  • Seller discloses garage door does not close fully and rainwater may enter garage.
Priority 04

Environmental proximity hits should be checked, not guessed.

  • NHD statutory hazard zones are largely "not in," but environmental databases show nearby hits.
  • Nearby EnviroStor, SLIC, LUST, Superfund/NPL, and RCRA records appear in the report.
  • Ask lender/insurer early whether any proximity flags affect underwriting.

Buyer action plan

這不是所有項目的清單，而是最值得先花錢查清楚的六件事。

1. Electrical quote

Ask a licensed electrician to evaluate service capacity, panel brand risk, attic wiring, grounding, GFCI, and safety-device corrections.

2. Roof and wood

Coordinate roofer and termite contractor findings. Roof age/end-of-life and wood damage can become one combined budget conversation.

3. Foundation retrofit

Get a foundation/seismic contractor to price anchor bolts, retrofit plates, crawlspace concerns, and moisture signs.

4. Plumbing scope

Evaluate galvanized/cast iron drain piping, low water pressure, bathroom leaks, water heater safety, and sewer line camera results.

5. Insurance check

Run the address through insurance early, including roof, panel, age, and environmental proximity questions.

6. Records check

Verify permits, square footage, lot size, boundary assumptions, and any repair documentation before relying on price-per-square-foot logic.

Agent operating notes

這一段是給買方經紀人的：少替專家下判斷，多把風險、文件、時間點和買方指示留清楚。

Receipt trail

Keep the 181-page package, delivery timestamp, buyer acknowledgement, question log, listing-side answers, contractor estimates, and any revised disclosures together.

Repair ask

Separate asks into life-safety, termite Section I, roof/electrical/foundation/plumbing, seller-disclosed garage water intrusion, and documentation/credits.

Contingency posture

If buyer shortens or waives contingency, confirm in writing that they reviewed the NHD, termite, inspection, TDS/SPQ/AVID, and report limitations.
