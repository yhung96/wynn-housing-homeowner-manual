Method

This workflow applies a land-heavy allocation model to nearby recent SFR sales.

1. Define comps

Use the nearest San Gabriel SFR closed sales within the past 3 months.

2. Split sale price

Use official assessed land and improvement value ratios for each comp.

3. Average unit values

Average implied land $/sqft and structure $/sqft across the selected comp set.

4. Apply to subject

Multiply averages by 7,749 sqft of land and 1,475 sqft of building area.
