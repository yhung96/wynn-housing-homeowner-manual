Method

This is the specific workflow used for land-heavy homes where the lot may drive most of the value.

1. Split each comp

Land share equals assessed land divided by assessed land plus assessed improvements.

2. Convert to unit values

Apply each share to sale price, then divide by lot sqft or building sqft.

3. Average the comps

Average land $/sqft and structure $/sqft across the selected comp set.

4. Apply to subject

Multiply averages by 15,245 sqft of land and 3,188 sqft of building area.
